## Detailed Requirements
1. **Workflow Pin in Preview (`submission.ts`)**:
   - When previewing a group function where `pin.kind === 'workflow'` and `pin.workflowRevision !== undefined`:
     - Resolve the workflow from the workflow registry / schema catalog for the requested function/endpoint.
     - Extract `slug`, `revision`, `digest` (and recipe pins if configured).
     - Include `workflow: { slug, revision, digest }` in `computeResolutionFingerprint`!
2. **Workflow Pin in Admission (`submission.ts`)**:
   - In `submitProfileV2`:
     - Recheck that the selected workflow revision is still active/published (not retired). If retired/changed, fail closed with 409 `PROFILE_CHANGED` (zero durable rows).
     - Include the resolved `workflowSchemaPin` in the admission call so that `compileAdmittedWorkflowPin` compiles and persists the workflow identity (`workflow_schema_slug`, `workflow_schema_revision`, `workflow_schema_digest`) and sealed recipe carrier on `operations` and `tasks`.
3. **Workflow Pin on Retry (`retry.ts`)**:
   - Ensure `retryOperation` preserves the original `workflow_schema_slug`, `workflow_schema_revision`, `workflow_schema_digest`, and reseals the recipe carrier for the new IDs under the universal lock order.
4. **Ordinary Functions Unchanged**:
   - Ordinary action functions (non-workflow) must continue to have `workflow: null` and remain unaffected.

## Acceptance Criteria
1. `tests/pc-r02-workflow-execution-pins.test.ts`:
   - Real v2 workflow preview/submit persists selected registry slug/revision/digest, producing expected compiled payload and recipe carrier.
   - Retiring/changing the workflow revision between preview and submit denies fresh admission with 409 `PROFILE_CHANGED` and zero durable execution rows.
   - Changing the selected workflow invalidates the preview (stale fingerprint).
   - Retry of a workflow operation preserves original workflow/recipe pins and reseals under lock.
