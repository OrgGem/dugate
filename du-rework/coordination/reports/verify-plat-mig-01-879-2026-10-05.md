### Identity expiry / refresh / user-gated — precise status:
- Expiry: `DU_CONNECTOR_IDENTITY_EXPIRES_AT` is a BOOT-time gate only. Past ->
  refuse boot; missing while headers set -> refuse boot; unparseable -> refuse.
  It is parsed by `connectorIdentityExpiresAtMs` and NOT passed to the connector
  and NOT used to re-issue anything. It bounds WHEN the boot may happen, not the
  lifetime of a per-request token.
- Refresh: there is NO refresh path. No hot-reload channel exists; the design doc
  (DESIGN-803) keeps a boot snapshot, so refresh = redeploy with a new identity.
  Nobody rotates the header automatically.
- User-gated: only the PROVISIONING of the identity (who may set the env and
  restart) is an operator action outside this code; nothing in the composition
  checks a role or writes an audit record for it. The READ side of the
  management surface is gated only by the existing admin session + tenant fence.
  So: "expiry enforced at boot" is real; "expiry/refresh is managed and
  user-gated in-app" is NOT.
