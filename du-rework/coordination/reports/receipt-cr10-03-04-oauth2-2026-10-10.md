## 2. Sua

CR10-03 - deadline va byte cap song song den HET body:

- Mot deadline cho CA giao dich (RFC 6749 §2.3.1 bounded wait): timer khong con bi xoa sau headers.
  Mot deadlineGate (Promise<never>) duoc race voi reader.read() nen co the ngat ca mot read dang treo,
  kể ca khi transport inject bo qua AbortSignal. clearTimeout + rejectOnDeadline = null nam trong
  finally bo nguyen phuong thuc.
- readBoundedBody(): doc stream, cong don byte theo tung chunk, vuot cap ngay lap tuc -> throw
  TOKEN_RESPONSE_TOO_LARGE; finally { cancelQuietly(reader) } giai phong socket trong moi nhanh thoat.
  Chi con fallback response.text() (khi double khong co getReader) van kiem tra kich thuoc cuoi.
- Cac nhanh tu choi (status / content-type / declared-length) goi releaseBody() (body.cancel()) truoc khi throw.
- Tach parseTokenResponse() de giu mot pham vi deadline duy nhat; khong doi semantics cache/retry/401.
- Ma loi van TOKEN_REQUEST_FAILED (khong doi union -> outbound-auth mapping khong doi), chi doi message
  phan biet 'did not complete within its deadline' vs 'unreachable'.

CR10-04 - transport mac dinh la pinned egress chung:

- Khong con global fetch: this.fetchImpl = options.fetchImpl ?? createPinnedFetch({...}) tu @du/egress
  (dependency workspace da khai san trong services/orchestrator/package.json; webhooks.ts:533 dung cung
  package). Adjudicate MOI DNS answer truoc connect, MOT resolution chung cho policy va connect (khong co
  cua so rebinding), dial dung IP da duyet, SNI/Host giu ten goc, redirect hop duoc adjudicate va khong bao gio
  bi follow. Khong sua gi trong packages/egress (chi import).
- Ba option moi (mirror cua webhook dispatcher): allowHosts (exact IP-literal opt-in), allowPrivateNetworks
  (RFC1918/loopback/ULA, KHONG bao gio metadata/link-local/CGNAT), resolveDns (resolver seam cho test).
  fetchImpl duoc ghi chu la seam THAY THE egress - caller tu chu destination policy khi inject.
- Ma loi moi TOKEN_ENDPOINT_DENIED: fail-closed, KHONG retryable, message khong chua secret.
  DestinationDeniedError cua @du/egress duoc map sang ma nay tai fetchTokenResponse().
- normalizeAllowHosts(): lowercase, bo bracket IPv6, chan gia tri rac.
