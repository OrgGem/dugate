### Actor and shared-session follow-up

Compose actor-forwarding design is approved in direction: BFF forwards exactly one already-validated du_session or du_admin cookie plus resolved CSRF; the internal route independently requires the platform bearer, removes Authorization while resolving that cookie, and rejects expired/forged proofs and invalid mutation CSRF. This denies normal local ADMIN Compose access. Backend/coordinator notified to require authenticated admin role and independent platform bearer without the extra tenant-null restriction; operator/viewer remain denied. Legacy undefined tenantId also triggered the original guard and must not be mistaken for a foreign tenant.

Shared API client now has a module-wide confirmed401 latch and CSRF epoch. Remaining races were sent to backend/coordinator: recheck latch/captured epoch after awaited CSRF fetch immediately before a protected mutation fetch; discard successful responses from a prior epoch, including after response.json; do not let a late session success clear a newer401. getSession currently always opts into recovery, including Overview periodic polling, so default session reads must respect the latch and explicit retry/reload should own recovery. These are in-flight fixes, not accepted behavior.

Backend/host have approved a runner-only host-scoped current-job read with expiry sweep, exact reconcile request replay, and manual UNKNOWN recovery that retains pending completion until successful reconciliation then marks it superseded.
