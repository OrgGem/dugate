## Approved financial semantics

All amounts are nonnegative safe-integer micro-USD at wire/storage boundaries and BigInt for arithmetic; USD formatting remains display-only. Commands refer directly to the **original initial charge event**, never another correction/refund event. Every accepted row has a monotonically increasing root adjustmentRevision and previous head reference, closing cycles/ambiguous branching.

- **Correction:** `targetChargeMicrousd` is the replacement **gross billable charge total before refunds**, not a delta to add. Preserve the original charge event and its original price snapshot; an authorized financial adjustment is a separate accounting basis. Latest accepted correction target wins through CAS, with old correction rows retained.
- **Refund:** `refundMicrousd` is a positive credit delta, subtracted once. It cannot exceed the root's current net charge. Refund records never have an unexplained negative runtime cost field.
- `initialCharge` is the immutable original amount; `grossCharge` is latest correction target or initialCharge; `refunded` is the sum of accepted refund deltas; `netCharge = grossCharge - refunded >= 0`. A correction below already-refunded total is rejected. Target equal to current gross is a no-op command result, not another billable row; refund must be greater than0.
- **Units/duration:** financial correction/refund does not change actual consumed input/output/cached tokens, pages or duration. Count the initial units once; adjustment metadata may show original units for attribution, but they are not new consumption. A financial refund cannot restore token quota. This packet implements monetary adjustment; an actual metering-unit correction would require separately typed validated measurement evidence and is not silently simulated by a refund.
- Every adjustment retains original tenant/key/profile/business/action/version/operation/task/invocation/attempt/Connector/provider/model attribution and initial price/provenance digest. Do not look up current Profile/Connector/model to rewrite history. A later published price revision may be cited as an additional immutable adjustment basis, but does not mutate the original price or automatically reprice all old events.

Minimal authority is an explicit currently authenticated platform Admin financial command, with bounded reason and an existing authorized source-evidence reference/content digest. The Admin approval is an auditable financial authority, **not a claim of provider-verified money**. Root eligibility requires an actual trusted priced committed initial event and completed original settlement where a reservation group exists. Reject pending/unpriced/unattributed/untrusted historical BASIC roots, ambiguous superseded provisional rows, unsettled/RUNNING/UNKNOWN groups, foreign tenant or missing source evidence. Preserve those records as unknown/unadjusted, not a fabricated zero; real migration evidence may later establish an eligible original. Known zero-price charges cannot receive a positive refund. Operator/USER/viewer and arbitrary Connector provider money cannot create credits. A future provider-origin adjustment needs its own verified authority/evidence workflow; it cannot enter via ordinary usage ingest today.

Keep original measurement/costStatus and pricing confidence; add `financiallyAdjusted`/adjustment authority/basis labels to the projection. Do not call an Admin-approved adjustment provider-measured or convert an estimated original into measured usage. Corrected amount is the approved accounting charge, distinct from the immutable provider/raw figure and frozen initial price calculation. Provenance/price change alone does not release quota or establish missing units.

## Scope, UTC periods, budgets and alerts

Adjustment effective attribution is the original root's immutable accounting time/window and resource dimensions, not the date the Admin clicks. Store original occurredAt/accounting basis and original reservation hold period pins where available; `recordedAt`/receivedAt is separately server-generated current time. Do not copy current time into effectiveOccurredAt to create current-period credit.

For each corresponding quota scope with a captured reservation-period pin, adjustments apply to that original UTC `[windowStart,windowEnd)`; otherwise reuse the committed root's validated original occurredAt window. Daily/monthly periods remain UTC. Original-charge window selection and adjustment selection must be one shared root projection; do not independently time-filter refund rows before loading their roots/head history. A delayed refund/correction restates the old accounting period and is visible in a separate recorded-time change view. It does not carry unused credits into a new period. Historical asOf views include only adjustments recorded by that asOf; original records remain exportable.

Budgets and legacy per-key spending use net monetary committed charge plus unchanged running/UNKNOWN holds; token/page policy uses original consumed units. An old-period refund cannot free current-period capacity; a current-period refund may lower that period's committed money, with same-lock concurrent admission ensuring no race/overcredit. No adjustment re-settles a group, modifies its settlement event ID, calls ordinary `usage.ingest`, or releases UNKNOWN reservations. All applicable parent+child budgets continue to apply. Existing alerts are immutable evidence of the condition when generated; add an accounting-change/re-evaluation result using the existing alert outbox if supported, without deleting past threshold alerts or faking delivery. Old period changes must not send a false current-period breach/recovery.
